About

Solar 13 is a professionally managed consultancy based in the Chandigarh tricity, focused on the renewable energy market in northern India.

We have worked on solar development in Punjab since the sector started in 2012, engaging with stakeholders and contributing to the formulation and implementation of solar and related fiscal policies.

We have also represented our clients before regulatory and judicial forums in Punjab and Haryana, and work closely with government departments and institutions including PSPCL.

Services

  • Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) facilitation
  • Land identification and acquisition
  • Government and regulatory liaison
  • Statutory approvals and clearances
  • Project development and execution
  • Commissioning and related approvals

Through our principals and associates we can also support end-to-end EPC where required.
